# Gremlins Film Characters

This document contains a list of known gremlin and mogwai characters from the Gremlins films (1984) and Gremlins 2: The New Batch (1990).

## Main Characters

### Gizmo
The adorable mogwai protagonist who remains good throughout both films.

![Gizmo](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/1/1f/Gizmo_%28Gremlins%29.jpg)

**Film Appearances**: Gremlins (1984), Gremlins 2: The New Batch (1990)

## Gremlins from Gremlins (1984)

### Stripe
The main antagonist gremlin with a distinctive white mohawk stripe. Leader of the first generation of gremlins spawned from Gizmo.

![Stripe](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/e/ed/Stripe_%28Gremlins%29.png)

**Characteristics**: Aggressive, intelligent, mohawk-like stripe of white fur
**Fate**: Killed by sunlight exposure

### Pete
One of the original gremlins spawned from Gizmo in the first film.

**Characteristics**: Member of Stripe's gang
**Fate**: Killed during the chaos in Kingston Falls

### George
Another member of the original gremlin group from the first film.

**Characteristics**: Member of Stripe's gang
**Fate**: Killed during the chaos in Kingston Falls

## Gremlins from Gremlins 2: The New Batch (1990)

### Brain Gremlin
A highly intelligent gremlin who gained enhanced intelligence after consuming brain hormone serum in the Clamp Center genetics laboratory.

![Brain Gremlin](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/f/f9/Brain_Gremlin.jpg)

**Characteristics**: Wears glasses, articulate speaker, intellectual
**Notable Quote**: "Civilization! I'll just step into the next century!"

### Spider Gremlin
A gremlin that injected itself with spider genetic material, resulting in a spider-gremlin hybrid.

**Characteristics**: Spider legs, web-spinning abilities, arachnid features

### Bat Gremlin
A gremlin that consumed bat genetic serum in the genetics laboratory.

**Characteristics**: Wings, bat-like features, flight capability

### Electric Gremlin (Mohawk/Daffy)
Originally called Mohawk, this gremlin had a mohawk similar to Stripe. Later transformed into the Electric Gremlin after being electrified.

![Electric Gremlin](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/gremlins/images/4/42/Electricgremlin.jpg)

**Characteristics**: Mohawk hairstyle (originally), electrical energy body (after transformation)
**Notable Trait**: Can travel through electrical systems

### Vegetable Gremlin
A gremlin injected with vegetable genetic material in the genetics laboratory.

**Characteristics**: Plant-like features, vegetable appearance

### Greta (Female Gremlin/Lady Gremlin)
A female gremlin created when a gremlin consumed female hormones in the genetics laboratory.

**Characteristics**: Feminine features, lipstick, sultry personality
**Notable Scene**: Attempts to seduce various characters

### Lenny
A slow-witted, goofy gremlin.

**Characteristics**: Dimwitted behavior, bulging eyes

### George (Gremlins 2)
A gremlin who drinks from a water fountain and multiplies.

**Characteristics**: Appears early in the Clamp Center invasion

### Phantom Gremlin
A gremlin wearing a phantom-style mask and cape.

**Characteristics**: Theatrical appearance, dressed like Phantom of the Opera

### Flasher Gremlin
A gremlin who wears a trench coat and flashes people.

**Characteristics**: Trench coat, exhibitionist behavior

## Other Mogwai (from Gremlins 1984)

In addition to Gizmo, several other mogwai were spawned in the first film before transforming into gremlins:

- **Unnamed Mogwai** (4 others) - These mogwai quickly transformed into gremlins, including Stripe and his gang

## Notes

- Gremlins are created when mogwai get wet and spawn offspring
- Mogwai transform into gremlins when fed after midnight
- Gremlins are destroyed by sunlight or extreme methods
- Gremlins 2 features many more mutated gremlins due to the genetics laboratory setting
