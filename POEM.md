# A Poem About Gremlins

In the digital realm where code runs free,
Live creatures of chaos, wild and spree,
The gremlins dance with mischievous glee,
Testing your app for all to see.

They click and they scroll through every page,
They type and they touch with boundless rage,
They fill out your forms with random stage,
Unleashing their chaos, age after age.

With clickers and typers, touchers so bold,
Scrollers and formFillers, stories untold,
They stress-test your code, both new and old,
Finding the bugs that must be controlled.

The mogwais watch over with watchful eyes,
Tracking the FPS, catching the cries,
Preventing the alerts, the app's demise,
Gizmo stops the chaos when errors arise.

So welcome the gremlins, let them run free,
They'll find every flaw in your UI,
Through random interactions, you'll come to see,
Your application's strength and stability.

For in the chaos, the truth is revealed,
Your code's hidden weaknesses are healed,
By gremlins and mogwais, forever sealed,
A stronger, more robust app is sealed.
